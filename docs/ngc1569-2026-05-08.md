  (0.81 vs 0.86). The Moon does not blur stars, so this is seeing, focus or the tube's temperature. 27 Oct was as
  soft (11–13 px). Check focus with a Bahtinov mask at the start of a night.
- **Sky** was 0.9 mag/arcsec² (2.2×) darker for NGC 1569: no Moon, even in twilight.
- **Depth**: the NGC 6503 stack is still ~1.2 mag deeper (3× lower noise per arcsec²), because it has 5.4× more
  exposure time. Dark current at 28 °C (70 e⁻ per 180 s frame) also adds ~40 % to NGC 1569's noise variance.
  NGC 1569 looks cleaner because it is sharper and its bright, compact starburst core shows detail at small
  scales. It is not lower noise.
