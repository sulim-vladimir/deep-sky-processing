## Findings

- Stars: ~8″ FWHM in single frames, ~6.5″ stacked (seeing at low altitude); ~3.5–5″ in the deconvolved galaxy.
- Flats were essential: 3 dust donuts, 2–3 % deep, from dust on the camera window ~4–5 mm in front of the sensor
  (donut width × f-ratio). Without flats they left dark rings, one beside NGC 6503.
- Offset 0 clipped 18 % of the dark pixels to the minimum. Calibrating without the master dark gave the same
  noise, so this did not cause the grain; offset 30 is used from now on.
- The stack is ~30 % noisier than √N predicts (no dithering) and the sky noise is mostly moonlight.
- 2×2 software binning gained only 1.2–1.3× (neighbouring pixels' noise correlated 0.43 by debayer + registration).
- Siril PCC left blue too strong; white balance from Gaia star colours instead.
- VizieR was down (HTTP 503 / timeouts) 26–28 Oct → plate solve via `WCS_FROM`.
